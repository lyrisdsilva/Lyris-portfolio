E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analysis

What drives sales, repeat purchases and late deliveries.

Category
Data
Built with
PostgreSQL, Python, pandas, Tableau
Key result
Tableau dashboard linking late deliveries to lower review scores

Overview

An analysis of 100K+ orders from the public Olist e-commerce dataset to understand what drives sales, repeat purchases and delivery delays.

The challenge

The data was spread across many related tables: orders, customers, payments, reviews and deliveries. Answering business questions meant joining it all reliably.

What I did

  • Wrote multi-table joins and window functions in PostgreSQL to build clean views of orders and customers
  • Used pandas to dig into sales drivers, repeat purchase behaviour and delivery timing
  • Built a Tableau dashboard of sales KPIs comparing review scores for late versus on-time deliveries

Outcome

Clear evidence of how delivery delays hurt customer reviews, and a set of recommended operational fixes for late deliveries.
