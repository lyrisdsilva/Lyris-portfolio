Booking site for outdoor team events.

Role
UI / UX design & front-end
Client
Sample client
Timeline
5 weeks
Tools
Figma, Next.js, CSS

Overview

A bright, simple website for booking outdoor team days, from picking an activity to paying a deposit.

The challenge

The old booking process lived in email threads. Organisers needed to compare options and book in one sitting.

What I did

  • Mapped the booking journey with the team
  • Designed activity cards that compare price, group size and duration at a glance
  • Built the front-end with accessible forms and clear error messages

Outcome

A booking flow organisers can finish on their phone during a coffee break.
