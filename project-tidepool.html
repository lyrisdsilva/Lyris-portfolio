Tidepool

A gentle habit tracker that rewards consistency, not streaks.

Role
Product design
Client
Sample client
Timeline
6 weeks
Tools
Figma, Rive, React Native

Overview

A mobile app that helps people build small daily habits without the guilt of broken streaks.

The challenge

Most habit apps punish missed days. The goal was a tracker that feels encouraging even after a rough week.

What I did

  • Interviewed early users about why they quit other trackers
  • Replaced streak counters with a tide that rises and falls with your week
  • Designed a calm visual language and micro-animations in Rive

Outcome

A clear, friendly app concept ready for development, with a full design system.
