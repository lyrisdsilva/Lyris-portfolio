Murmur

Voice notes that sort themselves.

Role
Prototype & interaction design
Client
Side project
Timeline
10 days
Tools
Claude, React, Web Audio

Overview

An AI-assisted voice notes prototype that groups your ramblings into ideas, tasks and reminders.

The challenge

Make capturing a thought as fast as talking, and finding it later just as easy.

What I did

  • Prototyped a one-button recorder with a live waveform
  • Used Claude to summarise and tag each note
  • Tested the flow with friends and cut every screen that slowed them down

Outcome

A working prototype that proved the idea and became a favourite demo.
