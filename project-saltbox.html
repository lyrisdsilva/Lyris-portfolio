Brand · 2025

Saltbox Bakery

Identity and ordering site for a neighbourhood bakery.

Role
Brand & web design
Client
Sample client
Timeline
4 weeks
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, Webflow

Overview

A warm, hand-made identity for a small bakery, carried through to a simple pre-order website.

The challenge

The bakery sold out by 10am every day. Customers needed a way to reserve bread without a complicated shop.

What I did

  • Built a wordmark and a set of bread-shaped icons
  • Designed a one-page pre-order flow that works in under a minute
  • Set up the site so the owners can update the daily menu themselves

Outcome

A brand that feels as warm as the shop, and mornings with fewer disappointed customers.
