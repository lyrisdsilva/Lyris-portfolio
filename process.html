My process

Small steps, shared often.

We talk, I sketch, we test, I build. You see progress every few days, never just at the end.

  1. We talk

    A short call to understand the idea, the people it's for and what success looks like.

    You get: a one-page brief

  2. I sketch

    Fast sketches and wireframes to explore directions before anything gets polished.

    You get: 2–3 directions to react to

  3. We test

    A clickable prototype we put in front of real people early, while changes are cheap.

    You get: a working prototype

  4. I build

    Design and front-end together, shared often, launched with care.

    You get: a live, tested product
