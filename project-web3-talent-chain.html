Web3 Talent Chain

Overview

A decentralized marketplace where people can post jobs, bid on tasks and earn crypto rewards, signing in with MetaMask.

The challenge

Freelance platforms rely on a middleman to hold payments and track reputation. The goal was to move that trust onto the blockchain.

What I did

  • Built the React.js front end with MetaMask authentication
  • Added job posting, task bidding and crypto rewards
  • Wrote Solidity smart contracts on an Ethereum testnet for automated escrow and milestone-based payments
  • Stored reputation on-chain

Outcome

A working marketplace where payments and reputation are handled by smart contracts instead of a middleman.
