Telco Customer Churn Analysis

Why customers leave, and which ones to save first.

Category
Data
Built with
SQL, Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Jupyter
Key result
Found a group that is 13% of customers but 34% of churn

Overview

An end-to-end churn analysis of 7,043 telecom customer records: who leaves, why they leave, and where a retention offer would pay off.

The challenge

Churn was spread across thousands of customers. The goal was to find the specific groups driving it and turn that into actions a business could test.

What I did

  • Queried the data with SQL (CTEs, window functions and conditional aggregation) and explored it in pandas
  • Found that first-year, month-to-month fiber customers were 13% of customers but 34% of all churn, about $53K in lost monthly revenue
  • Built logistic regression and gradient boosting models (AUC 0.85, 5-fold cross-validation)
  • Showed month-to-month contracts carry 3.5× and fiber service 2.4× the churn odds, and that the riskiest 20% of customers held half of all churners

Outcome

Five testable retention recommendations, plus a revenue-sizing scenario estimating a 3–6× return on a targeted retention offer.
